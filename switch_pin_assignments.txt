SWITCH MONITOR — PIN ASSIGNMENTS
Raspberry Pi 5 / MicroRave Project
Updated: 2026-04-25
================================================

GROUND
  All switch Commons (C) → daisy-chained → Physical Pin 6 (GND)

WIRING PER SWITCH
  Common (C) → GND rail
  NO         → GPIO pin (see table below)
  NC         → leave unconnected

SKIPPED / UNAVAILABLE GPIO PINS
  GPIO 7/8   — SPI0 CS0/CS1 (busy, claimed by kernel)
  GPIO 10    — Physical pin 19 missing from ribbon cable
  GPIO 14/15 — UART (reserved)
  Pin 20     — GND, also missing from ribbon cable

NOTES
  - Internal pull-up resistors enabled in software (no external resistors needed)
  - Switch OPEN  = pin reads HIGH (1)
  - Switch CLOSED = pin reads LOW  (0)
  - MicroRave does NOT use I2C
  - For final build: GPIO 10 (pin 19) can replace GPIO 2 for SW7 if full ribbon cable used
