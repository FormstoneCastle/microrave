SWITCH MONITOR — PIN ASSIGNMENTS
Raspberry Pi 5 / MicroRave Project
Hardware: Adafruit Perma-Proto Pi HAT (mounted directly on Pi 5 — no ribbon cable)
Updated: 2026-05-14
================================================

GROUND
  All switch Commons (C) → daisy-chained → HAT GND rail (any GND hole)

WIRING PER SWITCH
  Common (C) → GND rail
  NO         → GPIO pad on HAT (see table below)
  NC         → leave unconnected

REQUIRED PI CONFIG
  SPI must be DISABLED (frees GPIO 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 for general use):
    sudo raspi-config → Interface Options → SPI → No → reboot

UNUSED / SPARE PADS ON THE HAT (available for future expansion)
  TXD  (GPIO 14)  — Unused.  UART TX — disable serial in raspi-config first.
  RXD  (GPIO 15)  — Unused.  UART RX — disable serial in raspi-config first.
  CE1  (GPIO  7)  — Unused.  Already free (SPI is disabled).
  +5V rail        — Unused.  Left side of HAT.
  +3V rail        — Unused.  Right side of HAT.
  GND rails       — Shared with all switch Commons; extra holes still available.

DO NOT USE
  GPIO 0/1   — ID_SD/ID_SC (HAT EEPROM, reserved by HAT spec — never use)
  GPIO 26    — not broken out on the Perma-Proto HAT

NOTES
  - Internal pull-up resistors enabled in software (no external resistors needed)
  - Switch OPEN  = pin reads HIGH (1)
  - Switch CLOSED = pin reads LOW  (0)
  - MicroRave does NOT use I2C, SPI, or UART
  - All 22 pads chosen are labeled on the HAT — no under-HAT jumpers required
